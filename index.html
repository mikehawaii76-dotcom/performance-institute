The Science

Why every other clinic is doing it wrong

Injectable testosterone is the most prescribed treatment in men's health — and the most misunderstood. Understanding the physiology changes everything.

What everyone else prescribes

Injectable Testosterone (TRT)

Synthetic testosterone signals your brain to stop producing the real thing — shutting down your natural testosterone production, sometimes with long-term effects

Testicular shrinkage and infertility — your testes stop working because they're no longer needed

Markedly elevated blood counts (Polycythemia) — a common side effect that thickens the blood and requires regular therapeutic blood donation to manage, increasing stroke and clot risk

Accelerated hair loss and acne from supraphysiologic androgen levels

Weekly injections — forever. Managing needles, syringes, and supplies at home. Monthly trips to the pharmacy to pick up controlled substance prescriptions in person. You cannot stop without a difficult recovery process

FAQ

Questions men actually ask

What is the difference between your approach and injectable testosterone?

Injectable testosterone suppresses your body's natural testosterone production, which can cause testicular shrinkage, infertility, polycythemia, and accelerated hair loss in many patients. Dr. Michael's approach uses an FDA-approved oral medication that works differently — it signals your body to produce more testosterone naturally. Our patients routinely see an 80–100% increase in free testosterone within 30 days while preserving fertility and avoiding the common side effects of synthetic testosterone.

I'm already on TRT from another provider. Can I switch?

Yes. Dr. Michael regularly helps men transition from injectable TRT to our oral medication protocol. The process requires a careful evaluation and a structured transition — message Dr. Michael directly to discuss your specific situation.

What states do you serve?

The Performance Institute serves patients in Georgia, Texas, Tennessee, Arizona, and California — the states where Dr. Michael holds an active medical license. All consultations are conducted via telehealth (video or phone), and medication is shipped directly to your home. You must be physically located in one of these states at the time of your visit.

Does The Performance Institute accept insurance?

No. We are a cash-pay practice. Our $199/month membership includes up to 3 telehealth visits per month, medication shipped to your door, occasional medication refills, and lab oversight — with no insurance complexity or surprise bills.

How long before I notice results?

Most patients begin noticing improvements in energy, libido, and mental clarity within 4–8 weeks. Lab values showing testosterone increases are typically measurable at the first follow-up blood draw at 4–6 weeks.

I'm concerned about fertility. Is this approach safe?

Unlike injectable testosterone which causes infertility by suppressing sperm production, Dr. Michael's oral medication protocol actually stimulates your body's natural testosterone and sperm production simultaneously. This makes it uniquely suited for men who want to optimize testosterone while preserving or improving fertility — something injectable TRT simply cannot offer.
